# Tracks

The hackathon's three directions; each team must pick at least one. The idea brief claims tracks 1 and 3; fit re-assessed on 2026-11-17 after the checker-first pivot.

| # | Track | Organiser's examples | Our fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI for Cybersecurity | Phishing and scam detection, anomaly detection, deepfake detection, AI security assistant, fraud detection | **Yes** — the checker is scam and fraud detection: AI claim extraction, scam-language signals, document forgery check. Depends on [[scam-checker]] being built. |
| 2 | Cyber Defense & Digital Security | Network/application security, data protection, identity & access management, cloud security, threat monitoring | **Yes, and already working** — tamper-evident ledger, API role checks, AES-256-GCM for medical files. Not claimed in the brief. |
| 3 | Human-Centric Cybersecurity | Anti-phishing platforms, gamified cyber-awareness, digital security training | **Yes** — a public anti-scam tool for ordinary donors, with reasons shown for each verdict. Depends on [[scam-checker]] being built. |

## Why the pivot

Before the pivot the working product was a donation platform with one security feature (the ledger); both claimed tracks rested on unbuilt stubs and no AI existed in the code. Making the checker the product puts tracks 1 and 3 at the centre of the demo.

Final choice to declare at submission is pending: [[decisions]]. Options: 1 + 3 as in the brief, or all three since track 2 is backed by working code.
