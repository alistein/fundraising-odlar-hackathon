# Tracks

The hackathon's three directions; each team must pick at least one. The idea brief claims tracks 1 and 3.

| # | Track | Organiser's examples | Our fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI for Cybersecurity | Phishing and scam detection, anomaly detection, deepfake detection, AI security assistant, fraud detection | **Yes** — document forgery and fraud detection (edited, reused, AI-generated certificates and invoices) |
| 2 | Cyber Defense & Digital Security | Network/application security, data protection, identity & access management, cloud security, threat monitoring | Partial — encrypted medical files, role-based access, tamper-evident ledger; not claimed in the brief |
| 3 | Human-Centric Cybersecurity | Anti-phishing platforms, gamified cyber-awareness, digital security training | **Yes** — protecting donors from social-engineering scams (verify page, clone detection) |
