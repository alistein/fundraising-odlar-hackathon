# Pitch

Required format: 5 minutes pitch/demo + 2 minutes Q&A, 18 Nov 10:30–13:00. Section timings are fixed by the organisers; content is from the idea brief.

| Part | Time | Organiser's prompt | Our content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem | 45 s | What problem do you solve? | Fake campaigns took about 1M AZN from 36 families; since August a personal card accepts five donations a day. |
| Solution | 45 s | How does it work? | Clinic-verified campaigns, money direct to the clinic, public ledger. |
| Product demo | 2 min | Live demo of the working part | Create a campaign, clinic confirms, donate, see the ledger. Then submit a forged invoice and a cloned campaign and show both being caught. |
| Target user | 30 s | Who will use it? | Families raising money, donors, clinics and charities. |
| Impact / business potential | 45 s | Impact and application/business potential | Bank partnership for verified campaign accounts; expansion to community and corporate giving. |
| Future | 15 s | Next direction | Licensed payment partner, first clinic pilot. |
| Q&A | 2 min | Jury questions | Prep from [[swot]] weaknesses/threats |
