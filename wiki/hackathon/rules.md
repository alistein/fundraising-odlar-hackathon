# Rules

Participation rules from the official programme, and what they mean for the build.

- Teams of 2–5 people.
- Each team picks at least one of the three tracks. → [[tracks]]
- The solution must relate to a cybersecurity problem defined within the hackathon.
- A working MVP, prototype or demo developed **during the hackathon** is expected.
- Unauthorised access to real people's or organisations' systems, obtaining their data, and harmful activity are **forbidden**. All tests only in the environment permitted by the organisers.
- Day 1 is for building; day 2 for demo, pitch and judging. → [[schedule]]

## What this means for us

- Test payments only; no real bank, clinic or patient system touched.
- Synthetic patients, clinics, documents and card numbers in demo data.
- Forged-document and cloned-campaign demos use our own fabricated samples, not real campaigns scraped from social media.
