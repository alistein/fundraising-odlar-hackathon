# Card-to-card transfer limits (Azerbaijan)

The regulatory change that makes personal-card fundraising unworkable and gives the product its "why now".

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Effective | 1 August 2026 |
| Incoming transfers per personal card | max 5 per day |
| Card-to-card transfers per person | max 20,000 AZN per month |
| Higher limits | Only on a justified application to the bank |
| Stated purpose | Idea brief / news: transparency, less fraud. CBA Governor (29 Oct 2026): prevent "tax evasion and other illegal activities", incl. cards held in pensioners' names |
| Card-to-card volume | 66bn AZN in 2025; 48bn AZN in Jan–Sep 2026 (CBA Governor) |

**Limits confirmed** by CBA Governor Taleh Kazimov (APA, 29 Oct 2026); Trend describes them as cash-in limits *per payment instrument*. **Effective date 1 Aug 2026 still (unverified)** — inferred from early-August coverage; the official CBA text was not read.

**Contradiction:** the idea brief frames the purpose as anti-fraud; the Governor framed it as anti-tax-evasion and anti-illegal activity. Pitch wording: "the regulator wants money flows to be transparent and accountable", which is true either way.

## Why it matters

- A family collecting for an operation can now accept five donations a day; the usual channel is effectively broken.
- The regulator's goal is the product's goal — basis for the "regulatory tailwind" and "bank partnership" opportunities in [[swot]] (verified campaign accounts exempt from the cap).
- Expected workaround: spreading collections across relatives' cards and e-wallets.
