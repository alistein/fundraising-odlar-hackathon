# Card-to-card transfer limits (Azerbaijan)

The regulatory change that makes personal-card fundraising unworkable and gives the product its "why now".

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Effective | 1 August 2026 |
| Incoming transfers per personal card | max 5 per day |
| Card-to-card transfers per person | max 20,000 AZN per month |
| Higher limits | Only on a justified application to the bank |
| Stated purpose (Central Bank) | Increase transparency, reduce fraud |

**(unverified)** — figures come from news reports (Marja.az, Oxu.az, APA, Milli.az) and were not checked against the Central Bank's official text.

## Why it matters

- A family collecting for an operation can now accept five donations a day; the usual channel is effectively broken.
- The regulator's goal is the product's goal — basis for the "regulatory tailwind" and "bank partnership" opportunities in [[swot]] (verified campaign accounts exempt from the cap).
- Expected workaround: spreading collections across relatives' cards and e-wallets.
