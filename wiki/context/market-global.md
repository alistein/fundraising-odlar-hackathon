**Why banks pay now:** since Oct 2024, UK payment providers must reimburse most authorised-push-payment (APP) scam victims. Every scam that slips through costs the bank directly. Other regulators are moving the same way **(unverified beyond the UK)**.

## Expansion path (proposal, 2026-11-17)

1. **Azerbaijan** — beachhead: the free Tool, then the Platform and a first bank. → [[go-to-market]]
2. **Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Georgia** — same scam pattern, similar banking. The Tool only needs language and card-format support.
3. **API for banks and platforms worldwide** — "check this appeal / card / document" as a service inside banking apps (pre-transfer warning), crowdfunding platforms (campaign vetting) and messengers.
4. **Türkiye clinic network** — with the clinic stage, last on the [[roadmap]]. Verify the destination clinics once, then every source country benefits. **Network effect:** each verified clinic makes the registry more useful in every market.

**Identity layer abroad:** FIN code / myGov works only in Azerbaijan. Each new country needs its national e-ID or a KYC provider for the identity stage ([[roadmap]]).
