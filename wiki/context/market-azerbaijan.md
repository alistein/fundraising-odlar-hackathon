# Market: Azerbaijan

Size of the market, the channels scams run through, and competitors in Safefund's home market.

## Market facts

| Fact | Value | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payment cards in circulation | 20.6M (end Jun 2025); ~80% contactless | CBA via Trend | Official, second-hand |
| Bank-card transaction volume | >126bn AZN (2024, +27%) | CBA deputy chairman via APA | Official |
| **Card-to-card transfers** | **66bn AZN (2025); 48bn AZN in Jan–Sep 2026** | CBA Governor, 29 Oct 2026 | Official |
| Monthly cashless card payments | ~8–9bn AZN (autumn 2025), ~87% e-commerce | CBA via Report.az | Official, second-hand |
| Social media user identities | 6.73M (Jan 2025) | DataReportal | Estimate |
| Generosity | World Giving Index 2024: rank 119, down 65 places, the biggest faller | CAF | Index |
| Bone-marrow transplant in Türkiye | ~80,000 AZN quoted; $65k–$127k list prices | JAM News; Bookimed | Indicative |
| State-funded treatment-abroad slots | "About 20 a year" — a parent's claim | LinkedIn post | **(unverified)** |

**Read-out:** money already moves card-to-card at huge volume, and almost everyone is on social media, which is where the appeals live. Trust in giving is falling. Expensive treatment abroad means families have to fundraise.

## Where appeals and scams run

Instagram pages, TikTok, Facebook groups, Telegram and WhatsApp forwards. Each carries a child's photo, a story, and a personal card number. See [[fraud-cases]].

## Competitors and alternatives

| Player | What it does | Verifies cases? | Gap Safefund fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal cards on Instagram (status quo) | Family or "helper" posts a card | No | Everything; now capped at 5 transfers a day |
| layf.az (LAYF charity fund) | Claims a registered NGO that collects into its own account and pays hospitals, case by case | Claims "each case checked individually" | **(unverified)** — check whether it is live and its scale. No public scam checker, no ledger |
| Large foundations and the state treatment-abroad commission | Fund treatment through their own programmes | Yes, internally | Closed, not a channel for public giving; queues |
| Bank CSR and donate buttons (e.g. Karabakh Revival Fund in banking apps) | One-click giving to state funds | n/a | Not open to individual medical cases |
| SMS donations through mobile operators | Short-number campaigns | Depends on the NGO | No verification layer; partner, not competitor |
| layf.az (LAYF Xeyriyyə Fondu, registered NGO, 2021) | Donation platform: applicants upload ID and medical documents; admin checks manually in 1–3 working days; money goes to the foundation account, then to the hospital or institution | Yes, manual; no AI, no state identity check, no checks of posts elsewhere | Self-reported: ₼2.4M raised, 15K+ donors, 320 campaigns, "98% reaches beneficiaries"; report section empty, no audit **(unverified)**. Source: layf.az, read 2026-11-18 |
| GoFundMe and other global platforms | Some Azerbaijani families use them for treatment abroad | No ("cannot verify" in its terms) | Not local; no AZN, no clinic payout |
| Generic scam checkers (link and URL scanners) | Check URLs and websites | n/a | Don't read appeals, card numbers or medical documents |

**No local verified medical-crowdfunding platform and no appeal checker was found.** ICNL found no crowdfunding platforms at all in 2018. Re-check layf.az before the pitch.
