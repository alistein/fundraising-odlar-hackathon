# Fundraising law (Azerbaijan)

Who may collect public donations in Azerbaijan, and the structure Safefund uses to stay legal.

## Rules found

| Rule | Detail | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Only registered NGOs/NCOs may receive donations, not individuals | NGO Law (13 Jun 2000) | [[icnl-crowdfunding-2018]] | 2018 text |
| Every donation registered with the Ministry of Justice, under a written agreement | Cabinet Decision #336 (13 Nov 2015) | ICNL | 2018 text |
| Anonymous donations banned | Donor details go into annual reports to the Ministry of Finance | ICNL | 2018 text |
| Cash donations only up to 200 AZN, and only to charity-purpose NGOs | — | ICNL | 2018 text |
| Foreign grants and foreign service contracts must be registered | Tightened 2014–2025; fines and inspections added in 2025 | ICNL; Caucasian Knot (Aug 2025) | Press |
| Crowdfunding law No. 450-VIIQ, in force from Jan 2027 | Covers equity and lending crowdfunding under the CBA; **donation and reward crowdfunding are outside it** | Secondary guide | **(unverified)** |
| Card limits: 5 incoming transfers/day, 20,000 AZN/month per instrument | Applies to the personal-card collections families use today | [[card-transfer-limits]] | Official statement |

**Implication:** a family collecting on a personal card is legally grey and now capped. An NGO doing it by the book has to register every single donor. **Donation crowdfunding as GoFundMe runs it is practically impossible** (ICNL's words).

## Safefund's structure (proposal, 2026-11-17)

1. **The checker handles no money.** It is an information service, needs no licence, and launches first. → [[scam-checker]]
2. **Verified campaigns pay the clinic, not the family.** A donation is a payment toward a named patient's **clinic invoice**. The clinic receives it as payment for services, which is not a donation to an NGO.
3. **Money moves through a licensed partner** (a bank, or an e-money/payment institution) via its special-account product. Per ICNL, money received through an intermediary does not need per-payer registration.
4. **Optional foundation partner** for cases without one clinic invoice (travel, medicines): an existing registered charity NGO receives the funds; Safefund is its verification and transparency layer.

Needs a lawyer's confirmation before any real money moves. Tracked in [[open-questions]].

> "Under Azerbaijani law a private person can't legally run a donation drive, and since August a personal card takes only five transfers a day. Safefund doesn't fight the law — it routes money to the clinic through a licensed bank, which is exactly what the regulator wants."
