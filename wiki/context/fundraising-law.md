# Fundraising law (Azerbaijan)

Who may collect public donations in Azerbaijan, and the structure Safefund uses to stay legal.

## Crowdfunding law 450-VIIQ and Safefund (researched 2026-11-17, SAFEFUN-11)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Official text, number, dates | No. 450-VIIQ; adopted 14 Jul 2026, published 24 Jul, in force 24 Jan 2027. Statute text not read (Milli Məclis draft page unreachable; e-qanun.az not checked) |
| Is donation crowdfunding outside it? | **Yes in substance:** the law only regulates equity and debt crowdfunding. No exclusion clause names donations. One summary says donation models must sit outside the operator's regulated structure **(unverified)** |
| Platform requirements | LLC/JSC, CBA register before starting, 2,750 AZN fee, CBA-set minimum capital, governance and AML rules, 5-year records, reports, annual audit; fines under Art. 416-1 of the Code of Administrative Offences |
| Does Safefund's model (pay the clinic via a bank) fall under it? | No: it is not equity or debt. The NGO donation rules and the licensed bank partner still apply |
| Does the checker fall under it? | No: it handles no money and arranges no investment |
| Fit with the NGO donation rules | Unchanged: donations to NGOs still need registration. MP Bayramov (Aug 2026) said social campaigns are not "risky" card transfers and may need only advance notice to the bank (a statement, not regulation) |

**Pitch use:** Azerbaijan now regulates investment crowdfunding but has no regime for donation crowdfunding. That gap is what Safefund fills, without needing a CBA licence for the checker.

**Implication:** a family collecting on a personal card is legally grey and now capped. An NGO doing it by the book has to register every single donor. **Donation crowdfunding as GoFundMe runs it is practically impossible** (ICNL's words).

## Safefund's structure (proposal, 2026-11-17)

1. **The checker handles no money.** It is an information service, needs no licence, and launches first. → [[scam-checker]]
2. **Verified campaigns pay the clinic, not the family.** A donation is a payment toward a named patient's **clinic invoice**. The clinic receives it as payment for services, which is not a donation to an NGO.
3. **Money moves through a licensed partner** (a bank, or an e-money/payment institution) via its special-account product. Per ICNL, money received through an intermediary does not need per-payer registration.
4. **Optional foundation partner** for cases without one clinic invoice (travel, medicines): an existing registered charity NGO receives the funds; Safefund is its verification and transparency layer.

Needs a lawyer's confirmation before any real money moves. Tracked in [[open-questions]].
