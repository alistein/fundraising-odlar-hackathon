# Go-to-market

How Safefund launches in Azerbaijan and then grows abroad. Phase order follows the team's roadmap decision (2026-11-17, [[roadmap]]); timings are proposals. Market data: [[market-azerbaijan]], [[market-global]].

## Beachhead: Azerbaijani donors who see "help this child" posts

Everyone has seen these posts, and since August each one comes with more unfamiliar card numbers ([[card-transfer-limits]]). The first user is a **donor about to send money**. They need a free answer in seconds.

## Phases (Azerbaijan)

| Phase | When | Goal | Moves | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Launch the Tool (checker) | Q1 2027 | Become the habit "check before you send" | Free web checker + Telegram/WhatsApp bot (forward a post, get a verdict); Azerbaijani UI; seed the blacklist from public police reports | Weekly checks; share of checks returning scam/suspicious |
| 1. Platform | 2027 | Full product: campaign pages, donations through a licensed partner, public ledger | Start with **thalassemia and pediatric oncology** families (where the 1M AZN fraud happened); bank partner for verified-campaign accounts and a scam-card feed pilot | Campaigns on the platform; signed bank pilot |
| 2. FIN code / myGov identity | After Platform | Organisers proven to be real people (Azerbaijan only) | myGov integration; FIN code check on organisers and patients | Share of campaigns identity-verified |
| 3. Scale locally | After identity | Distribution | Mobile operators (SMS donations, scam warnings); corporate CSR giving | Banks on feed; corporates |
| 4. Clinic verification | **Last** | Clinic confirms diagnosis and cost and receives the payout | 1–2 Baku clinics, then Türkiye transplant/oncology clinics | Clinic-confirmed campaigns; AZN paid to clinics |

## Distribution channels

| Channel | Why | How |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / TikTok | Where the scam posts live | "Check before you send" short videos; partnerships with pages that share appeals |
| Telegram / WhatsApp bot | Appeals spread by forwarding | Forward → verdict in the chat |
| Media | Fraud cases are news (JAM News, Oxu.az, APA) | Give journalists a tool and data; publish an anonymised scam-trend report |
| Patient communities | Thalassemia and oncology parents are both targets and fundraisers | Parent groups, clinics' social workers |
| Regulator and police awareness campaigns | The MIA and CBA already warn the public about card fraud | Offer the checker as the action step in their warnings **(target, no contact made)** |
| Banks' apps | The moment before transfer | Pre-transfer warning from the scam-card feed |

## Expansion

Kazakhstan / Uzbekistan / Georgia → global API for banks and platforms → Türkiye clinic network (with the clinic stage). The Tool travels as-is; myGov doesn't, so each country needs its own e-ID or KYC provider. Detail and rationale: [[market-global]].

## Risks to GTM

- **Thin registry:** most checks return "unknown" at first. Lead with *scam* detection (blacklist, clones, forgeries), which works on day one.
- **Wrong verdicts** harm real families. Every verdict shows its reasons, and "suspicious" is the default when unsure ([[scam-checker]]).
- **Legal:** money only moves once a licensed partner and a lawyer sign off ([[fundraising-law]]).
