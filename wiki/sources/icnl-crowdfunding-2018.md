# ICNL: Research on crowdfunding in Azerbaijan (2018)

A legal review of how Azerbaijani non-commercial organisations (NCOs) can raise money online. Prepared for ICNL by Mahammad Guluzade (MG Consulting) and funded by USAID. File: `raw/icnl-crowdfunding-azerbaijan-2018.pdf`.

## Key takeaways

- The law has **no definition of crowdfunding**. In 2018 there were **no crowdfunding platforms** in Azerbaijan.
- **Only registered NCOs can receive donations, not individuals.** Each donation must be registered with the Ministry of Justice under a written agreement. Anonymous donations are banned. Cash is allowed only up to 200 AZN, and only for charity-purpose NCOs.
- The conclusion: crowdfunding through donations is "practically impossible". The workable route is to take the money as **taxable income** (simplified tax of 4% in Baku, 2% in the regions, for revenue up to 200,000 AZN a month) collected through an intermediary.
- Three workable channels: **SMS through mobile operators** (Azercell, Bakcell, Nar), **e-terminals** (MilliÖn, ExpressPay, E-manat; fees of 1–10%) and **bank special accounts**. Some banks waive the fee as CSR.
- Money received through an intermediary (a bank or operator) does not need per-payer registration, even from foreigners. Direct foreign payments to an NCO do.
