# Market and fraud research, 17 Nov 2026

Desk research for the business, market and GTM side of the pitch: fraud statistics (Azerbaijan, region, global), market size, legal setting and competitors.

**What it is:** web searches plus full reads of five pages, run on 17 Nov 2026. Not a single document; the URLs below are the sources. Press figures are second-hand unless the row says "official".

## Key takeaways

- **Azerbaijan has an official cyber-fraud baseline:** in 2024 the Interior Ministry (MIA) counted 22,442,838 AZN stolen in 16,804 cases, plus "more than 10,000" cases by other methods. Jan–Apr 2025: over 6M AZN. → [[fraud-statistics]]
- **Fake medical appeals are a repeat pattern, not a single case:** five or more people detained for collecting money with sick children's photos. The largest was about 200,000 AZN on Instagram (MIA, Dec 2025). → [[fraud-cases]]
- **Thalassemia case details confirmed:** criminal case opened Feb 2023; 36 children; ~1M AZN; three children died; trial in the Baku Grave Crimes Court (JAM News, 2 Jul 2024). → [[fraud-cases]]
- **Card limits confirmed by the CBA Governor (29 Oct 2026):** 5 incoming transfers/day, 20,000 AZN/month. The stated purpose is tax evasion and illegal activity, not "transparency and fraud" as the idea brief says. Card-to-card volume was 66bn AZN in 2025. → [[card-transfer-limits]]
- **Donation crowdfunding is legally hard for NGOs:** each donation must be registered with the Ministry of Justice and anonymous donations are banned (ICNL 2018). → [[fundraising-law]]
- **No local verified medical-crowdfunding platform found.** One candidate (layf.az) needs checking. → [[market-azerbaijan]]
- **Global:** scam losses of $442B a year (GASA/Feedzai 2025); fraud detection market $25–65B (2025); banks spend $21.1B on fraud prevention. → [[fraud-statistics]], [[market-global]]
