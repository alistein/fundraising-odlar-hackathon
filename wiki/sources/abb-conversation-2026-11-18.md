Informal feedback on Safefund from a project manager at ABB Bank (first recorded as a backend developer; corrected by Əli the same day). Reported by Əli in chat; no written record. This is one employee's view, **not ABB's official position**.

- He liked the idea and asked to see the video.
- Banks are working on lifting the 5-incoming-transfers-a-day limit for donation cards, but cannot do it comfortably because of fraud cases.
- Banks are looking for exactly this kind of solution: if the fraud problem is solved, they would be comfortable lifting the limit.
- Safefund could be integrated with banks to remove these problems.
