# Source: OYU CyberAI Hackathon 2026 — programme and judging

Official two-day programme (Azerbaijani) from Odlar Yurdu University's Startup and Incubation Center: tracks, schedule, judging criteria, pitch format, rules.

- Raw: `raw/hackathon-program.pdf`
- Ingested: 2026-11-17

## Key takeaways

- **Dates:** 17 Nov 2026 build day, 18 Nov demo & pitch day, at Odlar Yurdu University. → [[schedule]]
- **Organisers:** OYU Startup and Incubation Center; Department of IT and Cybersecurity.
- **Teams:** 2–5 people. **Output:** working MVP, prototype, or demoable solution.
- **Three tracks**; a team must pick at least one. → [[tracks]]
- **Submission 22:00–22:30 on 17 Nov** requires: product name, problem, solution, demo/MVP link, chosen track. → [[schedule]]
- **Judging:** 100 points over 5 criteria; emphasis on real working functionality, cybersecurity impact, technical execution. → [[judging-criteria]]
- **Pitch:** 5 min pitch/demo + 2 min Q&A, fixed section timings. → [[pitch]]
- **Rules:** no unauthorised access to real people's or organisations' systems or data; tests only in the organiser-permitted environment. → [[rules]]

## Notes

- The programme lists the opening ceremony 11:00–11:30 and Building Sprint I 11:00–14:00 — they overlap, so effective Sprint I is ~2.5h.
- Motto: "Təhlükəni müəyyən et. Həlli yarat. Gələcəyi qoru."
