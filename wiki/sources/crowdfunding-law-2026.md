# Crowdfunding law No. 450-VIIQ (2026)

Research into Azerbaijan's "Kraudfandinq haqqında" law, done 2026-11-17 for SAFEFUN-11. The official statute text was not read; this summarises a law-firm brief and press coverage.

## Key takeaways

- **Dates:** adopted 14 Jul 2026; published 24 Jul 2026; **in force 24 Jan 2027**. Law No. 450-VIIQ (number from press).
- **Scope: only equity and debt crowdfunding.** Equity crowdfunding means a joint-stock company issuing new shares. Debt crowdfunding means bonds of up to 5 years. The stated aim is financing for startups and SMEs.
- **Donation crowdfunding:** none of the sources show the law regulating it. They also found **no explicit exclusion clause** for donations, so donations sit outside the law's subject rather than being carved out by name. One legal summary says donation, reward and pre-sale models are not prohibited, but must run in a structure separate from the operator's regulated activity **(unverified)**.
- **Operators:** must be an LLC or JSC, entered in the Central Bank (CBA) register before operating, with a 2,750 AZN state fee. They also need:
  - minimum capital, set by the CBA
  - governance, internal-control and conflict-of-interest rules
  - AML customer due diligence
  - records kept for 5 years
  - reports and an annual audit
- **Campaigns:** 90 days maximum; at most 2 equity and 2 debt projects per owner per 12 months.
- **Investors:** a 7-day cooling-off period; funds returned within 5 business days if a campaign fails or is cancelled. The CBA sets investment limits.
- **Fines:** new Art. 416-1 of the Code of Administrative Offences.
- **MP Bayramov (Aug 2026):**
  - Aid, health and social campaigns are not treated as "risky" transfers under the card limits; the main requirement may be telling the bank in advance.
  - CBA-registered platforms will face no transaction restrictions.
  - The law "does not conflict" with the card rules.

## What it changes

- The open question "is donation crowdfunding outside 450-VIIQ?" is answered: **yes in substance**. No exclusion clause was found; the law simply doesn't regulate donations. → [[fundraising-law]]
- The NGO donation rules ([[icnl-crowdfunding-2018]]) still govern donations, so Safefund's structure stands: pay the clinic through a licensed bank.
- Bayramov's remark softens the [[card-transfer-limits]] story: social campaigns may get higher limits by notifying the bank. This is a statement, not regulation.
