## Key takeaways

- **One sentence:** a clinic confirms the patient's diagnosis and cost, donations go straight to the clinic, every payment is public — the organiser never touches the money and a fake campaign cannot collect. → [[product-overview]]
- **Why now:** since 1 Aug 2026 a personal card can receive max 5 transfers/day and 20,000 AZN/month. Card-number fundraising is effectively broken. → [[card-transfer-limits]]
- **Proven harm:** ~1M AZN taken from parents of 36 thalassemia patients. → [[fraud-cases]]
- **Six-step flow:** identify organiser → clinic confirms → documents checked → donations to clinic → public ledger → anyone can verify. → [[product-flow]]
- **Six threats, six controls.** → [[threat-model]]
- **Tracks:** AI for Cybersecurity + Human-Centric Cybersecurity. → [[tracks]]
- **MVP plan in three sprints** matching the hackathon schedule. → [[mvp-scope]]
- **Pitch outline** already matches the required format. → [[pitch]]
- **SWOT** → [[swot]]
- **Four open questions** (legal, competition, clinics, evidence). → [[open-questions]]
